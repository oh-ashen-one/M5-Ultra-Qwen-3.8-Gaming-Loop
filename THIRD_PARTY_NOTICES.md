## Before any future import

Record the upstream URL, pinned commit/release, exact files, author/copyright notice, full applicable license text, modifications, dependencies, and asset-specific terms. Preserve upstream attribution and notices in the distributed package. Do not apply this project's MIT notice over another component's license.

Unresolved asset/model/service rights block that component's import or redistribution until clarified. In particular, the GDQuest demo's noncommercial/share-alike art must not be treated as MIT art. Custom generated assets still require a documented rights review. No unresolved third-party component is bundled in this initial commit.
