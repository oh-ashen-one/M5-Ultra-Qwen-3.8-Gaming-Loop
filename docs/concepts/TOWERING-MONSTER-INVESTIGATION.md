# Towering-monster investigation concept

**Separate concept only.** This does not replace the urban local-loop brief, approve a new game, or start any generation/development. It records an original horror-investigation direction for later discussion.

The reference is cooperative investigation tension associated with Phasmophobia, combined with the physical presence of a towering, horned, Capra Demon-style creature. References describe mood and gameplay questions; no Dark Souls or Phasmophobia asset, character mesh, animation, level, sound or proprietary code is imported or licensed by that comparison.

## Physical threat changes the design

Build an original creature with its own silhouette, anatomy, equipment, animation and identity through local Qwen's original Blender authoring pipeline. No Meshy, Tripo or premade asset packs. Its scale should alter gameplay rather than merely replace a humanoid skin. Agree on dimensions first: height, shoulder width, reach, turning space, step height and which doors/passages it can use. The environment must support those constraints. This remains a separate discussion concept, not authorized game development.

- **Footprint:** a large body cannot fit through every door. Rooms, ceiling height, cover, line of sight and escape routes create different risks. Collision and navigation must agree with the visible silhouette.
- **Sound:** heavy footfalls, scraping horns/gear, breathing and impacts communicate distance and direction. Acoustic occlusion and material response matter; visual size alone does not create dread.
- **Pursuit:** approach, search, lost contact, chase, recovery and disengagement need readable rules. Turning/stopping time and attack telegraphs create fair escape opportunities instead of instant contact kills.
- **Environmental interaction:** the approved creature may push objects, strike doors, leave physical traces or force a route change. Author explicit interactable states and bounded destruction; do not promise arbitrary world destruction.
- **Investigation:** evidence should follow behavior and history—marks, disturbances, sound patterns, witness memory or changing room conditions—rather than be unrelated collectibles. Exact evidence types and win/fail structure remain open.

## Model-powered behavior and memory

The [NPC architecture](../NPC-ARCHITECTURE.md) applies: a model chooses legal high-level investigation/pursuit intents from permitted observations. The engine controls locomotion, animation, collision, attacks and sound. The monster may remember an observed hiding habit or a prior distraction, but receives no hidden exact player location and cannot fabricate knowledge to force a scare.

Persistent relationships could let an original creature become more wary, curious or hostile after attributable encounters. Each change needs a factual event and bounded rules. The video should demonstrate the causal chain—noise/trace, observed memory, selected legal behavior, visible consequence—without presenting invented internal thoughts as evidence.

## Questions for a future brief

Is the monster always physically present or does it manifest under defined conditions? Is the player identifying it, escaping, containing it or retrieving evidence? Single-player or cooperative? Which spaces are safe, which barriers can fail, and how can a player recover from a mistake? What does a complete short session end with?

Start any later qualification with one creature and one small original environment. Test doorway clearance, navigation/collision, sound cues, camera readability, attack timing, evidence logic and complete escape/failure/restart. This is a proposed test scope, not an authorized engine run or an approved final design.
